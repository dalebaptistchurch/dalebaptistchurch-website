How We Serve

Serving Together

We are a small church with a sincere heart for our congregation and our community. We don't do everything — but what we do, we do with genuine love for God and for people. Here's how we live out our faith together throughout the year.

"And let us consider how to stir up one another to love and good works, not neglecting to meet together, as is the habit of some, but encouraging one another."

Hebrews 10:24-25 ESV

Youth Forum

Every Wednesday evening our students gather for Youth Forum — a time for fellowship, Bible study, and growing together in faith. Open to middle and high school students.

▸ Wednesdays at 5:00 PM

Kid's Korner

Kid's Korner helps children understand biblical truth and learn how it applies to their everyday lives through engaging, age-appropriate activities and lessons.

▸ Wednesdays at 5:00 PM  ·  Kindergarten through 5th grade

Men's Prayer Breakfast

Every other month the men of Dale Baptist gather for breakfast, fellowship, and prayer. It's an informal but meaningful time — just men doing life together and bringing it before the Lord.

Women

Women's Ministry

Each year the women of Dale Baptist gather for a retreat — a special time set apart for rest, worship, and connection. We are always looking for ways to grow and add more opportunities for the women of our church to fellowship and encourage one another.

▸ Annual retreat + growing calendar of events

Outreach

Present in Our Community

We are a small church with a genuine desire to be present and available in our community. We don't make a lot of noise about what we do — we simply try to show up when it matters and love people the way Jesus loved us.

We are always looking for new ways to serve, and we welcome anyone who wants to get involved.

"In the same way, let your light shine before others, so that they may see your good works and give glory to your Father who is in heaven."

Matthew 5:16 ESV
Dale Public Schools Partnership

We are always looking for ways to partner with Dale Public Schools — to be present, available, and a blessing to students, teachers, and families in our community.

LifeWise Academy

Dale Baptist Church partners with LifeWise Academy by providing space each week for Bible classes serving local young people.

McLoud Food Pantry

We partner with and support the McLoud food pantry, and connect people in need with this resource when we can.

Always Growing

We may be small, but we have a big heart. If you have an idea for how we can serve our community better, we'd love to hear it.

We Are Always Growing

Our ministries are not a finished product — they are a living, growing expression of our faith. We are always praying about new ways to serve our congregation and our community. If you feel called to help lead or start something new, let's talk.
