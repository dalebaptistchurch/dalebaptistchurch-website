First Time Visiting?

Here's What to Expect

01

Come As You Are

There is no dress code, no expectation, and no pressure. Just come. You will be welcomed exactly as you are — because that's how Christ welcomes us.

02

Join Us for Worship

Sunday worship begins at 11:00 AM. If you'd like to ease in, Sunday School at 9:45 AM is a great way to get to know people in a smaller setting first.

03

Say Hello

We are a friendly bunch. After the service, introduce yourself to anyone — or reach out to us beforehand using the form above. We'd love to meet you.
