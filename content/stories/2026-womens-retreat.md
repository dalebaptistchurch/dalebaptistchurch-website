On April 17–18, 2026, nine women from Dale Baptist Church attended the Oklahoma Baptists Women’s Retreat at Falls Creek Conference Centers. Centered on the theme *Draw Near*, the weekend offered time for worship, encouragement, and hearing from God.

Most of all, the women enjoyed spending time together, strengthening friendships, and getting away for a fun and refreshing weekend.
