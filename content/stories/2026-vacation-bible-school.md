From June 15–18, Vacation Bible School was a great week at Dale Baptist Church! With the theme *Game On! Gearing Up for God!*, more than 30 children joined us for Bible lessons, activities, and plenty of fun.

Throughout the week, the children brought an offering for Oklahoma Baptist Homes for Children. Together, they raised more than $500—and earned the chance to put pies in the faces of some of our helpers!

This week would not have happened without the adults and youth who stepped up to serve. We are also thankful for everyone who contributed money, snacks, or drinks. Most of all, thank you to those who prayed for a successful week and for every child who attended.
