Dale Baptist Church has been richly blessed with a faithful church family, meaningful ministry, and a building that has served our community for many years. Through our *Blessed* Building Focus, we are prayerfully investing in what God has entrusted to us by addressing much-needed improvements such as sheetrock repair, new windows, and general maintenance.

More than anything, we ask for prayer that God will guide our decisions and provide what is needed. If He leads you to give, your contribution will help us care for this place and prepare it for the ministry still ahead.

## Replacing Our Windows

Many of the church's windows and surrounding frames have deteriorated after years of service. Replacing them will help protect the building, improve efficiency, and care for the place God has entrusted to our church family.
