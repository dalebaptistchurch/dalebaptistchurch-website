"For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand, that we should walk in them." — Ephesians 2:10 ESV

Our Purpose

Why We Exist

At Dale Baptist Church, everything we do flows from one conviction: that the gospel of Jesus Christ is the best news the world has ever heard, and we are called to share it. Our purpose — rooted in Scripture and lived out in community — shapes how we worship, how we serve, and how we welcome everyone who walks through our doors.

"Therefore, go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you."

Matthew 28:19-20 NIV

Our purpose is to:

Proclaim the Gospel

To proclaim and share the gospel of Jesus Christ with as many people as possible — in our church and throughout the world.

"And this gospel of the kingdom will be proclaimed throughout the whole world as a testimony to all nations." Matthew 24:14 ESV

Seek God's Will

To seek to do the will of God in our community as He empowers us through the working of His Holy Spirit.

"But seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added to you." Matthew 6:33 ESV

Experience Fellowship

To experience meaningful fellowship with God and with fellow believers — a community built on love, not obligation.

"Behold, I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, I will come in to him." Revelation 3:20 ESV

Grow in Christ-likeness

To help new and fellow Christians grow through total commitment to the Lordship of Christ in life, personality, and possessions.

"Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away; behold, the new has come." 2 Corinthians 5:17 ESV

Minister to Our Community

To be a church that ministers unselfishly to persons in our community in the name of Christ.

"There is one body and one Spirit — just as you were called to the one hope that belongs to your call." Ephesians 4:4 ESV

Welcome One Another

To be a community where every person — regardless of where they are in their faith journey — is genuinely welcomed as Christ has welcomed us.

"Welcome one another as Christ has welcomed you, for the glory of God." Romans 15:7 ESV

"For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life."
John 3:16 ESV

Our Doctrine

What We Believe

Dale Baptist Church holds to the historic Christian faith as summarized in the Baptist Faith and Message. These are not our opinions — they are the great truths of Scripture that have shaped followers of Jesus for centuries.

I

The Scriptures

The Holy Bible is God's inspired, authoritative Word — totally true and trustworthy in all that it affirms. It is the supreme standard by which all human conduct and belief should be measured.

2 Timothy 3:16-17 · Psalm 119:105
II

God — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit

There is one and only one living God. He reveals Himself as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — three persons of one divine nature, equal in every divine perfection. God is all-powerful, all-knowing, all-loving, and all-wise.

Deuteronomy 6:4 · Matthew 28:19 · 2 Corinthians 13:14
III

Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God. He was born of a virgin, lived a sinless life, died on the cross for our sins, rose bodily from the dead, and will return in glory. He is the only way to salvation.

John 1:1-14 · Acts 2:22-24 · John 14:6
IV

Salvation

Salvation is God's free gift, offered to all who repent and trust in Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior. There is no salvation apart from personal faith in Christ. Salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone.

Ephesians 2:8-9 · Romans 10:9-10 · Acts 4:12
V

The Church

A New Testament church is a local congregation of baptized believers who are committed to Christ and to one another. The church exists to glorify God through worship, fellowship, discipleship, ministry, and evangelism.

Matthew 16:18 · Ephesians 1:22-23 · Acts 2:41-47
VI

Baptism and the Lord's Supper

Baptism is the immersion of a believer in water — a public declaration of faith in Christ's death, burial, and resurrection. The Lord's Supper is a memorial of Christ's sacrifice, observed regularly by the church.

Romans 6:3-5 · 1 Corinthians 11:23-26 · Matthew 28:19
VII

The Kingdom of God

The Kingdom of God includes both God's general sovereignty over the universe and His particular kingship over those who willfully acknowledge Him as Lord. Christians are called to seek His Kingdom first in all things.

Matthew 6:33 · Luke 17:20-21 · Revelation 11:15
VIII

Last Things

God, in His own time and in His own way, will bring the world to its appropriate end. Jesus Christ will return personally and visibly. The dead will be raised — the saved to eternal life, and the lost to eternal separation from God.

John 14:1-3 · 1 Thessalonians 4:13-18 · Revelation 20:11-15
Dale Baptist Church subscribes fully to the Baptist Faith and Message (2000), the confession of faith of the Southern Baptist Convention. For a complete statement of our beliefs, read the full Baptist Faith and Message →

Our Connections

Our Affiliations

We are grateful to be part of a larger family of churches committed to the gospel. Our affiliations connect us to resources, partnerships, and mission efforts far beyond what any single congregation could accomplish alone.

The BGCO connects more than 1,700 Oklahoma Baptist churches for the purpose of sharing the gospel across Oklahoma and around the world through cooperative missions and ministry.

Visit bgco.org →

Pottawatomie‑Lincoln Baptist Association
